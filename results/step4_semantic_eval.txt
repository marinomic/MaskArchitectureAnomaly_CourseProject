 STEP 4 - EoMT-CITYSCAPES vs EoMT-COCO COMPARISON
 Task: semantic segmentation | Dataset: Cityscapes val (500 images)
 Evaluation resolution: 640x640 (same for both models)
========================================================================

THE COMPARISON PROBLEM
  The two models predict in different label spaces (COCO: 133 panoptic
  classes; Cityscapes: 19 semantic classes). For a fair comparison:
   - both models are instantiated at the SAME resolution (640x640);
   - the COCO predictions are remapped onto the 19 Cityscapes classes
     through a hand-defined mapping (COCO -> Cityscapes);
   - the SAME evaluation pipeline is used (same mIoU, same loader).

  Three readings of the COCO model mIoU are reported:
   - FAIR   : evaluated over all 19 Cityscapes classes
   - COMMON : only over the classes that COCO can actually map
   - ORACLE : upper bound with an ideal mapping assignment

OBSERVATIONS
  - The native Cityscapes model dominates on every class: expected, since
    it was trained exactly on that domain and label space.
  - The COCO collapse on classes like pole/traffic sign/train is largely
    STRUCTURAL: these classes have no clean counterpart in COCO panoptic,
    so the mapping cannot recover them.
