========================================================================
 STEP 8 - MASK-BASED ANOMALY SEGMENTATION BASELINES (EoMT)
 Benchmarks: SMIYC RA-21 | SMIYC RO-21 | FS L&F | FS Static | Road Anomaly
 Metrics   : AuPRC (higher is better), FPR95 (lower is better)
========================================================================

HOW THESE NUMBERS WERE OBTAINED
  Model : EoMT (mask architecture, DINOv2 backbone). Evaluated on all three
          checkpoints required by the assignment.
  Pipeline (see step8_mask_baselines.ipynb):
    1. The model is rebuilt directly from each checkpoint, inferring
       num_classes / num_queries / num_blocks from the weight shapes.
    2. Each image is passed through the model once. The mask architecture
       output (mask_logits, class_logits) is converted to per-pixel class
       scores via:
         scores_{c,h,w} = sum_q sigmoid(mask_logits_{q,h,w})
                                 * softmax(class_logits_q)_c
    3. From those per-pixel scores, four post-hoc methods are computed:
         - MSP, Max Logit, Max Entropy (as in Step 7)
         - RbA (Rejected by All): - sum_c tanh(f_c). RbA is only meaningful
           on a mask architecture, hence it appears here but not in Step 7.
    4. Same metric code as Step 7 (AuPRC / FPR95 from scratch in NumPy).
  The mIoU column reuses the Cityscapes-val semantic mIoU measured in
  Steps 4-5 for each checkpoint (it does not change with the post-hoc method).

OBSERVATIONS
  - Switching from a pixel-based model (Step 7) to the EoMT mask
    architecture sharply improves anomaly separation on the in-domain
    Cityscapes checkpoint: Max Logit and RbA reach AuPRC in the 60-90 range
    on SMIYC RA-21 / RO-21, well above ERFNet.
  - The COCO checkpoint is weak across the board (FPR95 near 100 on several
    benchmarks): without road-scene alignment the scores barely separate
    anomalies. Fine-tuning recovers a large part of this gap.
  - The best post-hoc method is checkpoint-dependent: Max Logit is the most
    consistent overall, RbA is strong on the Cityscapes checkpoint but
    collapses on the fine-tuned one, which is worth discussing in the report.
